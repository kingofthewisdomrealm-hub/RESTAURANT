RESTAURANT

A Covenant Builders training model. A restaurant is not a building with a kitchen in it. It is a building the state licenses, the fire marshal signs, and the utility can shut off. Build one by hand, in order, and meet everybody who has to say yes. Training — not a permit.

RESTAURANT The Six CallsAdvanced Ten gates and six judgment calls the code book does not answer · the seat count, the capacity charge, the interceptor size, who signs it, the sprinklers, and the order you call the inspections. You pick, the job carries on, and the bill finds you later. It ends with an itemised bill of what every call cost you in days and dollars, with the source under each line.
